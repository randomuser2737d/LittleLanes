# How to install Little Lanes

Little Lanes is free and works on phones, tablets and computers. You install it straight from its web page, so there is no app store. It takes about 30 seconds.

**Game address:** https://randomuser2737d.github.io/

## iPhone or iPad
1. Open the game address in **Safari**. (Chrome on iPhone works too on iOS 16.4 and newer.)
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open Little Lanes from your Home Screen once while you're online. After that it works offline.

## Android phone or tablet
1. Open the game address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install** if a banner pops up. If it doesn't, tap the **⋮** menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open it from your home screen or app drawer.

## Windows PC or Chromebook
1. Open the game address in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow). Or open the **⋮** / **…** menu and choose **Install Little Lanes**.
3. It gets its own window and appears in your Start menu or app launcher.

## Mac
- **Chrome or Edge:** click the install icon in the address bar.
- **Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer):** click **File** in the menu bar, then **Add to Dock**.

**Shortcut:** in the game, open **Settings** and tap **Install app**. It installs right away, or shows the steps for your device.

## Playing offline
After you've opened the installed game once with internet, you can play anywhere, including with no Wi-Fi and in airplane mode.

## Updates
Updates are automatic. When a new version is out, the game shows **"A new version of Little Lanes is ready"** the next time you open it with internet. Tap **Update**. Your game is saved first.

## Your progress
- Progress is saved on each device. It doesn't move between devices.
- Deleting the app deletes your progress on that device.
- If your device gets very low on storage, it may clear the game's saved data.

## Removing it
Remove it the same way as any app:
- **iPhone/Android:** long-press the icon.
- **Computer:** in the app window, open the menu and choose **Uninstall**.
